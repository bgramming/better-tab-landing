Instant Spatial Overview & Zero-Latency Search

Hit Ctrl+T or Alt+W. Your entire browsing universe appears as an elegant spatial grid. Type anywhere on the page to instantly filter through titles, URLs, window names, or tab groups in under 50ms.

Auto-Suspend RAM Saver

Reclaim up to 80% of your browser's RAM. Inactive background tabs are safely hibernated while preserving scroll positions and form inputs.

1-Click Duplicate Cleaner

Accidentally opened the same Reddit thread or GitHub repo 4 times across different windows? Better Tab detects duplicate tabs automatically and lets you clean them with one tap.

Audio Telemetry & Noise Hunter

Stop clicking through 50 tabs trying to find where that mystery video or music is playing. Live equalizer pills highlight noisy tabs instantly, complete with a quick 1-click mute button.

Save whole window sessions with custom tags. Sync your research and work stashes between your desktop and laptop via encrypted native browser storage.

Your browsing history and tabs belong to you. Better Tab runs entirely on your local machine with zero analytics tracking and zero server telemetry.
